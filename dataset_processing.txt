The dataset contains approximately 500 complete music recordings.

Each recording is approximately 8 minutes long and has:

one WAV file
one corresponding MIDI file
one era label

IMPORTANT:

The 8-minute recording/piece is the independent labeled sample.

The 12-second audio segment is NOT an independent dataset example. It is a temporal chunk extracted from a recording for processing by the audio model.

Do NOT randomly split 12-second segments between train, validation, and test sets.

This would cause data leakage because multiple segments from the same recording could appear in different splits.

The train/validation/test split MUST occur at the recording/piece level BEFORE extracting 12-second segments.

For example:

complete recordings
      |
      +---- TRAIN recordings
      |
      +---- VALIDATION recordings
      |
      +---- TEST recordings
                |
                ↓
         extract segments

Use a configurable recording-level split, with a reasonable default such as:

train = 70%
validation = 15%
test = 15%

The exact split must be deterministic when a random seed is provided.

Each recording is approximately:

8 minutes = approximately 480 seconds

The audio model operates on 12-second segments.

Therefore, divide each recording into consecutive 12-second segments.

For an approximately 480-second recording:

480 / 12 = approximately 40 segments

Use:

segment_length = 12 seconds

Do NOT change the model's 12-second input size.

For every 12-second segment:

12 seconds
     |
     +-- 0-3 sec
     +-- 3-6 sec
     +-- 6-9 sec
     +-- 9-12 sec

Each 3-second window is processed independently by pretrained Musicnn.

Therefore one 12-second segment produces:

[4, MUSICNN_FEATURE_DIM]

Then:

Linear(MUSICNN_FEATURE_DIM, 128)

produces:

[4, 128]

The four tokens MUST remain separate until after the Transformer.

Do NOT mean-pool the four Musicnn features before the Transformer.

If the recording length is not exactly divisible by 12 seconds:

Do not silently discard the remaining audio.
The final segment must be zero-padded to exactly 12 seconds.
The same rule applies to recordings shorter than 12 seconds.
A recording shorter than 12 seconds must be padded rather than discarded.

Keep the original WAV files unchanged.

Every extracted 12-second segment must retain its parent recording ID.

For example:

recording_001
    segment_000
    segment_001
    ...
    segment_039

The dataset item should preserve:

recording_id
segment_id
wav_path
midi_path
era_label

The era label of every segment comes from its parent recording.
